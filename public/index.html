About Us

Welcome to Udaya Hardwares

Legacy of Trust Since 1984:

Udaya Hardwares began as a small shop near Thingal Nagar Bus Stand and has grown steadily over four decades. Our journey reflects consistent dedication to quality, service, and customer satisfaction.

Expanding Across Kanyakumari District:

From our first expansion in steel to launching multiple showrooms in Nagercoil, Marthandam, Karungal, and Kesari Street, we've evolved to meet the growing needs of our customers. Each location is built to serve with convenience, variety, and professional support.

Comprehensive Solutions for Every Space:

We specialize in premium Tiles, Sanitaryware, Kitchen fittings, Hardwares, Plumbings and more — everything needed for the perfect finish. Our showrooms are designed to inspire, offering a curated selection backed by expert guidance.
